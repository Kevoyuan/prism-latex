\section{What the pane gets right}

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\i
  \item \textbf{Paper, not a screenshot.} Pages keep their aspect ratio, a hairline border and a
        quiet shadow, so they read as sheets rather than images.
  \item \textbf{State you can see.} A dot and one line report compiling, ready or failed; a
        two-pixel sweep runs along the top while a document recompiles, and the old pages stay put.
  \item \textbf{Source that reads.} Commands, braces and comments are tinted just enough to scan,
        with a tabular line gutter.
\end{itemize}
